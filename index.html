        Choosing a response
        
          
        Fixed
        You changed your code. Say what you changed and why it solves the problem, then send your updated code: the finding should disappear.
        
          
        Justified
        The issue is real, but acceptable in your project. Explain why, and what risk remains. At least 15 words.
        
          
        Rejected
        The tool is wrong about this. Explain how you know. At least 15 words.
        
        
        What the labels mean
        
          
        Awaiting your response
        You haven't chosen Fixed, Justified or Rejected yet.
        
          
        Waiting to confirm
        You marked it fixed. Send your updated code to confirm it's gone.
        
          
        Needs attention
        Your reason is too brief, or you marked it fixed but it was still found in your latest code.
        
          
        Complete
        You've given a full response.
        
        
        Good reasons look like this
        
          
        "Justified: CORS allows every origin only while I develop locally with the Angular dev server. The deployed API reads its allowed origin from an environment variable."
        
          
        Specific, about your project, and shows you understand the risk. "Not relevant" or "fine" isn't a reason.
        
        
        Good to know
        
          
        Your responses stay on this device
        They're saved in this browser only. Download them to keep a copy and to submit.
        
          
        No findings doesn't mean perfect
        Automated checks recognise known patterns. Your README's security section is where you show your own judgement.
        
          
        Use of AI
        You can use AI tools, but you must understand and be able to explain every change you make.
